Tailoring & Proportions

Size & Fit Guide

Every SUREA piece is engineered for graceful posture and unrestricted natural stride. Follow our guidance below to find your dog's ideal proportions.

How to Measure Your Companion

Use a soft, flexible dressmaker's tape measure while your dog is standing squarely on a flat, even surface. Keep the tape comfortably snug against the coat without pulling tight:

1. Back Length

Measure from the base of the collar (at the nape of the neck) along the curve of the spine to the base of the tail where the tail joins the body. This is the primary measurement for coats and sweaters.

2. Chest Girth

Measure around the deepest, widest part of your dog's ribcage, immediately behind the front legs. Allow approximately two fingers of ease under the tape for breathing comfort.

3. Neck Circumference

Measure around the base of the neck where a collar naturally rests. Ensure room for two fingers to slide underneath comfortably.

Need Bespoke Sizing Guidance?

Each dog's silhouette is unique. If your companion has an unusually deep chest (such as a Whippet or Dachshund) or broad shoulders (such as a French Bulldog), our dedicated concierge team is delighted to review measurements and advise on the most flattering fit.

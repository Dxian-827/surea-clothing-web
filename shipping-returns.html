Care & Delivery

Shipping & Returns

We ensure every piece arrives safely and gracefully. Transparent shipping terms, complimentary threshold delivery, and 30-day effortless returns.

Delivery Methods & Timelines

Orders are fulfilled Monday through Friday from our central British atelier and regional dispatch hubs in North America. Orders placed before 1:00 PM local time are dispatched the same business day.

Service Destination Estimated Transit Cost
Complimentary Heritage Shipping United States & United Kingdom 4–7 business days Complimentary on orders $100+ ($10 otherwise)
Royal Express Courier United States & United Kingdom 1–2 business days $15 flat rate
International Priority Canada, Europe, Australia 5–9 business days $25 flat rate

Presentation & Packaging

Because opening a package should feel like unboxing a bespoke gift, all SUREA orders are wrapped in unbleached tissue paper, sealed with our gold wax crest emblem, and nestled inside a sturdy recyclable presentation box finished with forest-green grosgrain ribbon.

30-Day Complimentary Returns

We want you and your dog to be completely confident with every piece. If the size, drape, or style is not perfectly suited, we gladly accept returns and size exchanges within 30 days of delivery:

  • Pieces must be in original unworn condition with all tags and protective sleeves attached.
  • Please try on garments over a clean coat to prevent hair transfer prior to confirming size.
  • We provide prepaid domestic return shipping labels for all customers in the US and UK.
  • Refunds are credited to the original payment method within 3–5 working days of inspection.
